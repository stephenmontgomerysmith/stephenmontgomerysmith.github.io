Stephen Montgomery-Smith, Novel Washout Filters to Enhance Simulation of Motion. Presented to the Human Research Program Investigators’ Workshop, February 7-9, 2023, Galveston TX. We are seeking a physical simulator of a vehicle driven on the surface of a celestial body. This will be added to a virtual reality simulation. We desire that the motion of the simulator be so that the acceleration forces experienced by the vestibular system are similar to if the crew member were to actually drive the vehicle on the surface of the celestial body. But the simulator is constrained, first in its position in that it cannot travel far, and second in its orientation in that it must approximately face the same direction. (link to poster)
